# Inspect publication history and determine what configuration is running

## Outcome

Authorized users can inspect retained submitted configurations and determine what
is currently running, including after a browser disconnect or a new login. The
console accurately distinguishes recorded publication outcomes, unknown outcomes
and configuration faults so users do not mistake uncertainty for failed activation.

## Requirements

- Add retained publication history and snapshot detail to the embedded console,
  using Phase 3's protected inspection APIs. Show submissions in their authoritative
  order, recorded status, durable local/source identity when present and the current
  runtime-published snapshot. Do not treat the intended SQLite pointer as evidence
  of current execution or framework generation IDs as durable snapshot identities.
- Viewers, editors and admins may inspect all retained submitted content, including
  pending and failed attempts and authored sensitive values. Preserve full skill
  YAML, routes, targets and authored placeholders; render them safely as text.
  Private unsubmitted drafts and validation results remain visible only to their
  owning session, including when the inspecting user is an admin.
- Present pending status as outcome-unknown. A currently running snapshot may have
  unknown bookkeeping; do not silently relabel its stored history as succeeded.
  Failed submitted content is still inspectable. Explain runtime/intended mismatch
  and mutation faults, distinguishing failed activation from successful activation
  followed by bookkeeping failure. Show the actual mutation restrictions and direct
  users to documented operator recovery when intervention is required.
- Make history reachable from the authoring/publication workflow and after
  reconnecting or logging in again. Users can inspect the current runtime snapshot
  and retained submissions without restoring a lost private draft. Do not claim to
  prove an old operation's outcome when recorded evidence is insufficient, and do
  not automatically retry publication or offer a cancellation action.
- Handle concurrent publication and retention pruning without combining IDs and
  content from different snapshots. Explain when requested history has expired and
  offer access to the refreshed retained list/current configuration. Do not invent
  missing outcomes or retain extra history to make browser links permanent.
- Honor management permissions, CSRF where relevant and idle-session behavior.
  Background inspection/polling does not renew editing ownership or keep an idle
  login alive. Provide keyboard access and textual status labels alongside color.
- Use existing snapshot inspection and publication state; do not introduce a
  durable job/operation-status system. Export/import, rollback controls and
  destructive transfer confirmation belong to Phase 5. Supply this ticket's
  browser verification and user/operator documentation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Authorized viewers can browse retained submissions and inspect complete
  authored skills/routes/targets, including pending/failed content, sensitive
  literals and placeholders, safely rendered. Submission ordering and local/source
  identities are preserved; other sessions' private drafts/results are inaccessible.
- [ ] The UI separately identifies runtime-published and intended snapshots,
  recorded history status and mutation faults. Pending remains outcome-unknown even
  when that snapshot is active; activation and bookkeeping failures are distinguished
  without inferring undocumented outcomes.
- [ ] After disconnect/relogin, users can reach history from the console and inspect
  current state without recovering their lost private edits, blindly republishing
  or cancelling an accepted update. Fault guidance describes restrictions and links
  to the documented operator recovery procedure.
- [ ] Concurrent publication/pruning produces consistent snapshot detail or explicit
  absence. Expired entries lead to a refreshed list/current view, without mixed
  content, invented outcomes or a new retention/operation-status subsystem.
- [ ] Real authenticated browser workflows verify role access, keyboard/textual
  feedback and session expiry despite background inspection. Relevant inspection,
  publication and security regressions pass against the installed snapshot.
- [ ] User/operator documentation explains submitted history versus private drafts,
  retention, unknown outcomes, current versus intended state and fault recovery.
  Phase 5 transfer/rollback controls are not introduced by this ticket.
