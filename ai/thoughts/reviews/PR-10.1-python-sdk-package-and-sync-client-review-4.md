## Open Questions and Assumptions

The supported ordinary SSL context remains stable during use. For an EOF-delimited
HTTPS response, completion requires the peer's TLS closure alert. This lets a
length- or chunk-framed response complete by its framing and prevents accepting a
truncated close-delimited response on an abrupt TCP disconnect.
