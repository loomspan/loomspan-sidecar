## Findings

### [P2] Clear stale runtime content when current inspection fails
- Location: `src/main/resources/static/management/assets/console.js:200`
- Scenario: A viewer loads Current, then presses Refresh while `/api/management/configuration/current` returns 503. Before the fix, the old snapshot and its runtime/intended fields remained displayed under the current-runtime heading while only the status line reported failure.
- Impact: A user investigating a publication or mutation fault could mistake the old display for fresh evidence of what is running.
- Evidence: The new `failedCurrentRefreshDoesNotPresentStaleRuntimeAsCurrent` browser regression failed before the fix: after a simulated 503, `#current-content` still contained the previous runtime UUID and configuration. `loadCurrent` previously replaced content only on a successful response; its catch updated only `#current-status`.
- Fix: Clear `#current-content` and announce loading before the inspection request. Retain the error status if it fails; render the new snapshot only on success.

No findings remain after the fix and internal re-review.
